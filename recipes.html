Beyond the plate

Three uses people do not expect

In the marinade, not on top

A few tablespoons in a buttermilk or oil marinade seasons the whole piece of meat instead of just the surface.

Stirred into mayo or sour cream

Instant sauce for sandwiches, fries and fish tacos. Start with one part sauce to four parts base.

Over cream cheese

Pour Amy’s Sweet Heat over a block of cream cheese, serve with crackers, watch it disappear.
