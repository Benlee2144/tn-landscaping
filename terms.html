Product information

We describe every sauce as accurately as we can, including the peppers it is built on and where it lands on our heat index. That index compares our sauces to each other; it is not an industry standard and it is not a Scoville rating of the finished bottle.

Pepper heat varies from harvest to harvest, so the same sauce can be slightly hotter or milder between batches.
