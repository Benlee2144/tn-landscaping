Our story

It started with one habanero sauce and a stubborn recipe

Ichiban was the first bottle we ever made, and the recipe has not changed since. Everything that followed came from the same question: can we make this hotter, or milder, without giving up what makes it worth eating?

Six sauces later, the answer is a lineup that covers the whole table — the green one for weeknight tacos, the cajun one for the seafood boil, and the scorpion-mango for whoever keeps saying nothing is hot enough.

How it's made

Four steps, no shortcuts

Source

Whole peppers picked for the profile we want — serrano for backbone, habanero for lift, scorpion when the sauce calls for it.

Blend

Small batches, cooked and balanced by taste. Seasoning blends are mixed in-house rather than bought in.

Bottle

Filled and capped by hand, batch by batch, so nothing sits around waiting on a production run.

Label

Every bottle gets its number, its peppers, and its place on the scale. You always know what you are picking up.
