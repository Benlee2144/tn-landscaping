Six sauces out of one kitchen

We are a small-batch hot sauce maker in Knoxville, Tennessee. Everything starts with whole peppers and ends with a label applied by hand.

Where it started

Ichiban came first, and the recipe never changed

The first bottle we ever made was habanero based. We were not trying to build a lineup — we were trying to make one sauce that was worth keeping on the counter. That sauce is Ichiban, and it goes out today on the same recipe it did the first time.

Everything after it came from the same two questions. Can we take this flavor somewhere milder without making it boring? And can we take it hotter without losing the flavor underneath?

The answers turned into Zaxe for the everyday bottle, Ryn’s Mellow Verda for the green side of the table, Ragin Cajun for the seafood boil, Shine the Light for heat with a ginger finish, and Amy’s Sweet Heat for the people who kept telling us nothing was hot enough.

How we work

Small batches, on purpose

Making more at once would be easier. It would also mean cooking to a schedule instead of to taste, and that is the part we are not willing to give up.

Peppers first

Every sauce is named for the pepper that drives it — jalapeño, serrano, habanero, scorpion. That pepper decides the whole build.

Seasoned in-house

The cajun blend in Ragin Cajun is ours. So is the ginger balance in Shine the Light. Nothing gets bought in pre-mixed.

Cooked to taste

Peppers change through the season, so batches get adjusted rather than measured blindly against a sheet.

Bottled by hand

Filled, capped and labeled here. Batch sizes stay small enough that nothing sits waiting on a production run.

Knoxville, Tennessee

An East Tennessee sauce, made for East Tennessee food

These sauces were built around the way people here actually eat — barbecue, fish fries, Sunday breakfast, tailgates, and a lot of things that come out of a fryer.

You will find us at markets and shops around Knox County, and we ship anywhere in the country for everybody else.
